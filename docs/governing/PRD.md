## 1. Purpose and scope
TruckBill is a unified multi-tenant logistics, transport operations, fleet and accounting SaaS. Broker, OwnFleet and Partload are workflow mechanisms within one company workspace, not separate applications. This is an independent new codebase. Legacy applications are excluded as sources for code, architecture and feature reuse; they may be used later only for data migration after the new platform is ready. Reports are deferred until underlying workflows are implemented.

**Out of scope (DEC-005):** Partload Expense Management is explicitly excluded from Partload. OwnFleet expense management (including trip expenses/diesel and any separately defined OwnFleet expense add-ons) remains in scope and must not be removed because of this exclusion.

**Coding gate (DEC-004):** Phase 0 formal approval prerequisite is **satisfied** (PO-APPROVE-001, 21 November 2026). Application coding must still follow `Phases.md` sequencing. Phase 1 engineering foundation may be planned under approved boundaries only when separately authorized by the Product Owner; this document approval alone does not start Phase 1 implementation in the repository.

## 3. Identity and login
One global identity with mutually exclusive Internal Team or Customer type. One login system; separate authorization boundaries. No public self-registration. Internal users onboarded by Internal Super Admin. Customer users invited from Internal CRM leads. Reuse existing identity; never overwrite password.
Customer identity may own multiple companies and belong to multiple companies. Branch access is explicit selected branches; no “All Branches”. New branches do not grant existing users access. Owner automatically accesses all company branches, including future branches. Owner can add users from Owner Panel with name, email, mobile, password, company/branch and branch-specific role. Existing identity password is never overwritten. Branch creation selects existing users and roles; does not create identities. Branch edit does not manage users. No forced password change at first login.
Customer single active session across web and TruckBill mobile app; new login invalidates previous. Internal Team may have multiple sessions. Password change/deactivation invalidates customer sessions. Ownership transfer invalidates new Owner sessions; old Owner remains logged in but loses access to transferred company.

**Email changes (DEC-009):** Email address changes for Internal and Customer identities may only be performed through authorized administration. There is no self-service email-change functionality. **Pending:** identity verification steps, notification recipients, uniqueness enforcement details, and audit field requirements for administrative email change.

**Account deletion (DEC-006 / DEC-003):** Users cannot delete their own accounts. Account lifecycle changes use authorized administrative processes and applicable identity, membership, deactivation, soft-deletion and audit rules. Hard deletion and restoration of identities are prohibited.

## 4. Company Owner and company lifecycle
Exactly one Company Owner per company, a protected designation separate from ordinary roles. Only Internal Team Super Admin/Admin can transfer ownership to an existing Customer User. New Owner receives Owner privileges and ordinary membership for that company is removed. Previous Owner loses all access to transferred company; other company access is unaffected. Other users' memberships/branch assignments/roles remain unchanged. Transfer history retained.
Internal Super Admin/Admin can deactivate/reactivate Owner and company. These are **distinct** lifecycle actions (DEC-002).

**Owner identity deactivation:** A deactivated Owner identity cannot log in. This is not company deactivation and does not by itself define company Branch/Owner Panel behaviour.

**Company deactivation (DEC-002):** Does not deactivate the Owner identity. Branch Panel access is blocked for the Company Owner and all company users of that company. The company remains visible as Deactivated. **Owner Panel remains accessible** for the active Owner (login-capable identity with Owner designation) and may provide company-level administrative and billing information. Company deactivation must not restore operational Branch Panel access. Company data and history are preserved (deactivation is not deletion). Users can access other active companies. Authorization must not treat deactivated company status as a blanket deny that removes this Owner Panel exception (see Architecture §3).

If ordinary access revocation/ownership transfer leaves a user with no memberships, soft-delete identity and invalidate sessions. Exception: do not soft-delete solely because Owner/company deactivation removed access. Hard deletion and restoration of companies, branches and identities are prohibited (DEC-003). **Pending:** exhaustive list of Owner Panel actions permitted while a company is Deactivated (do not invent beyond administrative/billing visibility already stated).

## 5. Owner Panel / Branch Panel
Owner Panel exclusively accessible by Company Owner. It manages companies, branches, users, company settings, subscription and billing. Customer Admin and other roles cannot access these areas; operational role permissions do not grant Owner Panel.
Owner always lands in Owner Panel. Owner selects company/branch to enter Branch Panel and can return without new login. Non-owner with one branch goes directly to Branch Panel; multiple branches see branch selection. Customer permission changes are immediate with server-side enforcement/cache invalidation.

## 11. OwnFleet Trip
One OwnFleet LR = one Trip; no separate trip creation screen. One vehicle and one driver for entire trip. Vehicle selection auto-selects primary driver. “Use Substitute Driver” selects trip-only substitute; does not alter vehicle assignment.
Pickup: Station, Consignor, Goods, Weight, Pickup Date. Drop: Station, Consignee, one/more selected Pickups, allocated weight per pickup, Rate, Freight, Distance, Package, EWB, Goods Value. Pickup may allocate across multiple drops; drop may include multiple pickups. Every pickup's allocations must exactly equal original before completion; partial allowed Pending/In Progress. Allocation >0; rate may be zero; per-allocation freight may be zero but total freight across allocations >0. Distance/package/EWB/Goods Value optional. Distance manual per pickup-drop; automated routing deferred. Only Pickup Date; no Drop Date.
Dispatch Pending→In Progress; starting odometer mandatory; no comparison with last vehicle odometer. Departure date/time optional; blank defaults dispatch timestamp, entered value preserved. Completion requires ending odometer > start, actual arrival required and editable/default current; distance=end-start. Expected end optional; if set Early/On Time/Delayed, otherwise N/A; company timezone.
Cancel Pending/In Progress only by Owner/Admin, regardless of income/expenses. Cancellation date/time/reason required; no cancellation odometer. Cancelled remains visible; income/expenses retained. Soft delete separate, Pending only, explicit Delete permission; may delete with expenses; excludes LR/Trip from normal lists/reports/operational calculations/profit but retains expenses/income/docs; no restore. In Progress/Completed/Cancelled cannot soft-delete.
Expenses multiple: category/date/amount, optional payment mode, paid-from Bank/Cash account, remarks. Diesel requires litres/rate per litre/odometer; amount = litres × rate. Trip mileage = distance/fuel reliably attributable else Not Available; no estimate. Vehicle mileage estimated from consecutive diesel entries without full/partial tank distinction, may fluctuate, does not alter configured mileage. Planned diesel quantity/rate optional; planned amount if both; planned efficiency optional; no planned distance. Trip profit = agreed LR freight + additional charges − expenses; GST not revenue.
Multiple POD per LR, optional after delivery; view/download; replace/delete with remark/audit; not required to complete. Multiple supporting docs with Document Name, Type, File, Upload Date, Uploaded By.

## 12. OwnFleet Invoice Engine
One completed LR individual invoice; multiple completed uninvoiced LRs same customer consolidated. Separate particulars = each LR line; Combined = freight one line and references in annexure. Custom positive charges with description (detention, demurrage, halting, loading surcharge, other), included in invoice/GST.
Additional charges editable only Pending/no payments; permanently locked after first payment; edit requires remark/audit.
Payments multiple/partial: Amount Received, TDS 194C, Shortage/Damage Deduction, Discount (reason mandatory), Payment Date, Mode, optional Reference, Fill Net. Settlement = Amount Received + TDS + Deductions. Cannot exceed remaining balance. Cumulative TDS <= base freight. Payment immutable after save. Status Pending/Partial/Paid.
Invoice TDS 1% Individual/Transporter, 2% Company/Corporate; base freight excludes GST/additional charges; distinct from transporter TDS. GST follows company config and snapshots.
Cancel only if no payment; reason mandatory. Cancelled/deleted unpaid LR may be re-invoiced if eligible. Invoice numbering separate from LR; Auto/Manual.
Deletion: latest only in scope, Pending/no payment; second-last only after latest deleted. Shared Data latest across company; Restricted latest within branch. Number may be reused after soft delete; retain original record/audit, new internal ID; active-only duplicate check.

## 13. Broker / Market-Hired
Flow: market vehicle Bilty → Lorry Hire → transporter vouchers → delivery with Memo/POD → post-delivery adjustments → customer invoice.
Transporter freight per goods row, mapped to LR goods row; rate type/rate/amount; Fixed Amount override.
TDS rules: Owner valid declaration and <=10 goods vehicles = 0%; >10 standard rate. Agency ignores declaration and uses standard rate by PAN/entity type. Invalid/inactive PAN 20%. Active PAN 4th char P/H 1%; C/F/A/B/J/L/T 2%. Manual override remark. April 1 declarations reset No and standard rate recalculated. TDS uses original loading weight/fixed freight; unloading adjustments do not change basis. Submission lock affects TDS only. Store basis/rate/amount per Lorry Hire. Rule engine structured AND/OR, priority, active/inactive; no match is config error; changes new transactions only; snapshots retained. Lorry Hire stores Owner/Agency type.
Vouchers: multiple advance/final, partial. Modes Bank, Cash, Paid to Pump, Adjust in Advance. Bank/Paid to Pump Pending; Cash/Adjust in Advance In Progress. Daily page defaults today, multiselect; Pending→In Progress→Paid. Bulk only same Transporter and Vehicle; each voucher separate amount; common mode/type/date/remark. Transporter Book Balance auto-updates.
Lorry Hire cancellation only Owner/Admin; preview freight/TDS/adjustments/payments/outstanding; ask settle outstanding: yes transfers applicable amount to Transporter Book Balance; no retains against cancelled Lorry Hire. Reason/audit. Active Lorry Hire cannot delete; cancelled Lorry Hire auto-soft-deleted if associated LR soft-deleted; active Lorry Hire blocks LR deletion.
Bilty cancel only after Lorry Hire cancelled; delivered cannot cancel. Pending-only soft delete, active Lorry Hire blocks; cancelled Lorry Hire auto-soft-deleted with Bilty. In Progress/Delivered/Cancelled cannot soft-delete.
One POD/Memo number may attach multiple Bilties; bulk assignment for same customer; each Bilty individual delivery record and shared association; multiple attachments.
Adjustments: detention adds transporter balance; deviations add transporter balance; misc expenses deduct transporter balance; unloading weight recalculates customer/transporter freight using lower of loading/unloading unless fixed; shortage/damage deduct transporter balance and customer invoice. Multiple unloading adjustments preserve old/new weight/date/time/user/reason; original loading unchanged; latest unloading used; TDS original. Multiple shortage/damage preserve type/quantity/rate/amount/date/reason/creator/time.
Invoice only after delivery/adjustments. Select Billing Customer. Sections: delivered with POD/Memo (multi-select, show numbers) and without POD/Memo (individual). Both can be same invoice for same Billing Customer; prevent duplicates.
Linked Bilty: group number auto company/branch sequence; minimum two; shared Date/Vehicle/Payee/Driver/Reference. Each independent Bilty has LR No, Billing Customer, GR No, Invoice No, Consignor, Loading Station, Consignee, Unloading Station, Goods Type, units, weight/unit, Goods Value, Weight, Rate, Amount. Lorry Hire per Bilty before voucher payment. Group payment proportionate to each Lorry Hire outstanding after TDS, adjustments, previous payments; no over-allocation; separate voucher/payment per Lorry Hire, common mode/date/remarks.

## 19. Subscription and Billing
Product-level subscription behaviour is summarized here. **Detailed commercial rules, catalogues, proration, discounts, quotas, credits, Razorpay webhook behaviour and invoice-number approval** are specified in **Rules §15** and must not be reinvented in the UI or application code.

- One company billing account and one common renewal date; no base/workspace fee.
- First-company trial per Owner: 15 days; subsequent companies: no trial.
